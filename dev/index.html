AshDieback

The mechanistic-statistical model of the spread of ash dieback (Hymenoscyphus fraxineus) across France of Fritsch, Grosdidier, Gégout-Petit and Marçais, with its data and its Bayesian inference: a Julia port of PEOC (Parameters Estimation Of Chalara model).

A yearly cycle of spore production from colonised leaf debris, spore diffusion and saturation (the rachis colonisation model) drives a Bernoulli symptom model for each tree, observed through a binomial survey model. See AshDiebackProblem to simulate it, loglikelihood to score it, AshDiebackPosterior for the posterior as a LogDensityProblems.jl density, and ComponentwiseGammaMH and amis for PEOC's samplers.

source

AshDieback.jl implements the mechanistic-statistical model of the spread of ash dieback (Hymenoscyphus fraxineus, "Chalara") across France of Fritsch, Grosdidier, Gégout-Petit and Marçais, Mechanistic-statistical model for the expansion of ash dieback (HAL 04690647). It is a port of PEOC, the authors' Python code, and is tested against it.

The model

The model runs on 16 km quadrats with a yearly step and has three nested parts.

  1. Rachis colonisation (deterministic). Leaf debris (rachises) colonised in the two previous years, Ra = χ{a−1} + χ{a−2}, produce spores νa = Ha Ra above an Allee threshold r and Ha Ra²/r below it, with Ha = β₀ + β₁ ha and ha the June rainfall. The spores diffuse for τ = 60 days, ∂w/∂t = D Δw + νa/τ with zero-flux boundaries, and the newly colonised rachis is χ_a = min(w(τ), S) d, with d the ash density.
  2. Symptoms (Bernoulli per tree). A tree develops symptoms in year a with probability q̃a = (χ{a−1}/d) f(T{a−1}) / rS, where f(T) = ([1 − T/γ]₊)^κ penalises hot summers, and keeps them with probability Cpers: qa = q̃a (1 − Cpers q{a−1}) + Cpers q_{a−1}.
  3. Observation (binomial). The number of trees with symptoms among the 30 × visits observed in quadrat i in year a is Binomial(30 × visits, q_a^i).

The first two years are imputed from smoothed prevalence maps. The ten parameters are described in AshDiebackParameters.
