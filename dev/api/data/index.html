AshDieback.load_peoc_data — Function
load_peoc_data(dir = datadir(); temperature_index = :T28, climate_years = 2007:2022,
               observation_years = 2008:2023, grid = QuadratGrid())

Read the PEOC input files from dir and put them on grid.

Reproduces PEOC's parsing exactly: coordinates are rounded to quadrats with ties to even, Corsica is dropped, cells without data are zero, survey quadrat indices are shifted by the west and south borders, and repeat visits to a quadrat are merged in file order. The arrays are bitwise equal to PEOC's.

temperature_index chooses the climate column used for T: :T24, :T26, :T28 (the paper's choice), :T30 or :T35.

AshDieback.PEOCData — Type
PEOCData

The input data of the model on a QuadratGrid, as read by load_peoc_data.

Fields:

  • grid::QuadratGrid
  • climate_years::UnitRange{Int}: the years of rainfall and temperature (third index).
  • rainfall::Array{Float64,3}: June rainfall ha (mm), size `(nx, ny, length(climateyears))`.
  • temperature::Array{Float64,3}: the temperature index T_a (days in July and August with a maximum over the threshold), same size.
  • temperature_index::Symbol: which index temperature holds (:T24, :T26, :T28, :T30 or :T35).
  • ash_density::Matrix{Float64}: smoothed ash basal area d (m²/ha); zero in the border.
  • psi::NTuple{2,Matrix{Float64}}: the smoothed prevalence maps Ψ used to impute the colonised rachis of the two years before the first simulated year.
  • observations::Vector{YearObservations}: the survey, one entry per year.

AshDieback.YearObservations — Type
YearObservations

The disease survey of one year, aggregated by quadrat.

Fields:

  • year::Int: the survey year.
  • x::Vector{Int}, y::Vector{Int}: grid indices of the visited quadrats.
  • proportion::Vector{Float64}: mean proportion of trees with symptoms over the visits.
  • visits::Vector{Int}: number of plot visits; each visit observes observed_trees trees.
  • visit_x, visit_y, visit_proportion: the individual plot visits behind the means, in file order, as rmse uses them.

Repeat visits to a quadrat are merged in file order with a running mean, as PEOC does.

AshDieback.QuadratGrid — Type
QuadratGrid(; quadrat_length = 16.0, nx_mainland = 62, ny_mainland = 61,
            border = (3, 3, 3, 3), x_origin = 55_000.0, y_origin = 1_708_000.0,
            x_limit = 1_047_000.0)

The regular grid of square quadrats covering mainland France on which the model runs.

Quadrats are located by extended Lambert II coordinates in metres. The mainland covers nx_mainland × ny_mainland quadrats of side quadrat_length km whose south-west corner quadrat is centred at (x_origin, y_origin). border = (left, right, bottom, top) adds fictitious quadrats with no ash on each side, which moves the zero-flux boundary of the diffusion away from the coast. Points with x ≥ x_limit (Corsica) are not on the grid.

Arrays on the grid have size size(grid) = (nx, ny) and are indexed [x, y], west to east and south to north. See quadrat_index.

AshDieback.quadrat_index — Function
quadrat_index(grid::QuadratGrid, x, y)

The (i, j) index (1-based) of the quadrat containing the point with extended Lambert II coordinates (x, y) in metres, or nothing if the point is in Corsica (x ≥ x_limit).

Coordinates are rounded to the nearest quadrat centre with ties to even, as numpy.round does in PEOC, so the indexing of the input files is reproduced exactly.

AshDieback.GBInputs — Type
GBInputs

The 10 km squares of Great Britain with land, from load_gb_inputs.

  • squares: (easting_km, northing_km) of each square's south-west corner.
  • ash_ha: modelled ash area in the square, in hectares.
  • seeded: whether ash dieback was first confirmed in the square in 2012–2015. The model's epidemic starts in these squares.
  • t28_years, t28: days over 28 °C in July and August from HadUK-Grid, squares × years.

AshDieback.load_gb_future — Function
load_gb_future(inputs::GBInputs; dir = joinpath(datadir(), "gb")) -> Dict

Days over 28 °C from CHESS-SCAPE, bias-corrected, for model years 2026–2080, rounded to 0.01 days: (scenario, member) => squares × years, for "rcp45" and "rcp85" and members "01", "04", "06", "15". The few squares without a value take their nearest square's. CHESS-SCAPE years are model years, not real ones, and RCP4.5 is scaled from RCP8.5.

AshDieback.gb_problem — Function
gb_problem(inputs::GBInputs; B = 25.0, theta, temperature = nothing, last_year = 2100,
           final_year = last_year, initialization_year = 2014, border = 10, seed = 1,
           delta_t = 1.0) -> (prob, cell)

The model on Great Britain, with no survey: an AshDiebackProblem to pass to HostProblem, and cell(easting_km, northing_km), which gives a square's (i, j).

  • Ash density in a square is ash_ha / 100 / 100 * B, with B the stand basal area in m²/ha.
  • The epidemic is seeded in initialization_year in the squares first found in 2012–2015, with theta's C_init as their share of symptomatic trees.
  • theta defaults to the paper's θmax with C_init = 0.05.
  • Temperature is HadUK-Grid to 2025. After that it is whole years resampled from 2004–2025, or with temperature (a matrix from load_gb_future) that matrix to 2080, then whole years resampled from its 2071–2080. Years past 2080 are for the code's horizon, not results.
